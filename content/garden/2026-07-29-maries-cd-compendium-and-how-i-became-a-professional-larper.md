Hello everyone, welcome to the secret garden. This is probably the first time you've seen this area, unless you've been stalking my pages, then this probably shouldn't be too unfamiliar to you. Regardless, welcome. I'm no stranger to creating websites out of boredom, so this really shouldn't be any different, but the main difference is that I want it to host all of my writings, past and future, away from any other websites created that isn't wholly my own. I've seen that most posts are made using Google Docs, or by using alternative websites such as Medium, which is all fine and good, whatever gets the job done is really what's most important, but I've always been someone who takes the "extra" route. There's so much more that I have yet to update on here, I was so focused on all my other projects that for this step, I've pretty much neglected so much of it. There will eventually be other things to do here, there's a whole other side to the site that just hosts my personal study material, but it really isn't meant for others to see yet. In terms of content, you really only have the markdowns disguised as posts (where I tried to mimic the feel of Amino in a lot of aspects) as well as the ability to leave comments if you wish. The downside of the latter is I have to use my admin abilities and approve them manually, which really sucks, I hate giving myself more work, so maybe don't leave any comments? It's a double-edged sword. 

These days, every time I post, I disappear for another year or so. It can't really be helped. I had someone recently describe me as someone who "loves to work" but that's really not the case—it's all a means to an end for me. I work 9 days a week, which I know sounds impossible, but that's probably the best way to describe my situation. I work 9-5, Mon-Fri as an admin assistant. Then, Thur-Sun, I work at the ice arena still, and whenever the days intersect with my other job it simply means that I continue working from the early evenings to the night. The weekends are typically an all-day ordeal depending on whether I party hostess, which means I'm a glorified waitress every now and then, but the tips are good, very good. Then, during the days where I don't work double shifts, I have classes. I have taken my degree so slowly over the past years due to juggling my jobs and having had a more prominent social life, so I had taken to doing two classes a semester or taking unnecessary gaps, and I know that if I truly locked in, I could finish everything no problem, but I've just been in a dilemma of sorts. It started first when I had turned 18 and I needed to provide for my family more, and while I still do, I realized I also needed to start seriously saving if I'm going to be more independent in my life. So, even though I tell myself all the time that I need to quit the rink so I can have more free time that just belongs to me, I plan on hitting  20 grand first before I consider that, which I've already made great progress in doing, so fingers crossed on eventually making that a reality. 

So let’s finally get into the core of the post. This post features my collection of CDs which is in part, one of many other things that I do collect. My car doesn’t have a CD player, which may seem like it’d drill a hole in my ability to listen to them for the most part, but I came up with a solution to that. Rather than buying a cheap player and hooking it up to my car, why not drop a cool hundred dollars on a FiiO DM13 just so my autism dreams of wanting to see a disc spin as it plays get fulfilled. So that’s what I did. And nowadays, I hoard all of my CDs in my glove compartment for easy listening. It’s the perfect larp, anytime I have anyone in my car, I let them choose any CD they want and play on any one of my homebrewed video game systems that I never played. I do play my PS Vita, though. It’s the best handheld ever created.

Now, here’s the compendium of all of the CDs that I do own, and with each one, I attached my review of it, big and small, as well as a star rating alongside each one that I deem worthy of it.

 You can call me Marie. Here’s what I have to say.

* Avril Lavigne - Let Go 

  * In the past, I haven’t been too honest about how I felt about this album, since I actually received it as a gift so I kind of have to feign my enjoyment of it. But this shit is so ass. The only reason most people would seek out this album would probably be for Sk8r Boi, which whenever I listen to it makes me think of how I’m listening to formulaic music from an industry plant. Besides that track, it’s drowned out by music that sounds like it belongs on the Disney channel— I truly never cared for Hannah Montana growing up, but that’s exactly how it comes across even if it probably wanted to deviate from that sort of image.
* Boston - Boston

  * 70’s rock slop. Good music for when it’s your first day on Earth and you want to be cultured on music that is considered culturally acceptable and well-liked enough. Sucks in every other facet.
* Collective Soul - Dosage  ★

  * That cover art is so cool, and the disc art is pretty incredible too. I got it because it has Heavy, but the rest of the album stands on its own as pretty decent. 
* Daft Punk - Random Access Memories  ★

  * I don’t really like sucking on the nuts of big artists, but this is actually a pretty good album. It’s the one with the Get Lucky slop song which thankfully is near the end of the album, and it’s not a bad song in any technical sense but it’s been marred by how overplayed it used to be, or maybe still is, the radio actively annoys me so I don’t play it. My favorite song is probably Giorgio by Moroder by how unconventional it starts in the beginning, and I’ve heard it was a meme once upon a time just recently, which just further goes to show time is just a concept to me and I’ve always been under a rock towards most things. 
* Daisuke Ishiwatari - Guilty Gear Sign Original Soundtrack  ★

  * It’s ironic that I have this album since I don’t really care for fighting games, but I do really like Guilty Gear for anything but the actual game. The artstyle of Sign and Xrd Rev 2 is what really drew me to the franchise alongside the music, which I’ve always listened to on occasions outside of this album. I’ve only listened to Disc 4 so I can’t really give my opinions on the other 3 discs, but the fact that it has so many discs and divides it based off of what you’re going for (I only cared to listen to the music with lyrics) is pretty cool! The discs themselves has really cool art, and I’m not even touching upon how cool the cover looks. It’s just a great collectible for dedicated Guilty Gear fans, something that I’m not, but I try my best to pretend to be.
* Eminem - Curtain Call: The Hits  ★

  * It’s a very good low cortisol Eminem album to throw on, but who in their right fucking mind decided that “Fack” was a hit song worthy to be on this album and no less, be arguably the first song you listen to (ignoring the intro “song”). It’s such a shitty song that I unironically like to believe it was organized that way on purpose, so as to not dock my rating on this.
* Eminem - The Eminem Show

  * Same deal but it has less notable tracks. I honestly don’t remember much from this album besides Without Me, s’okay overall.
* Eminem - The Marshall Mathers LP  ★

  * It has Dido so that’s all that really matters.
* Fuel - Hemorrhage (In My Hands) \[single]

  * Analogous to the title, it felt like I had hemorrhaged in my hands when I bought this CD for $7.99 and it only featured one song. Doesn’t feature any B-sides, remixes, nothing, just had the one song and the fanfare was already over. And what sucks was that the song is really good, just like (most of) Fuel’s discography, but it felt like it was all over before it really got started. This CD made me want to go back to the record store that I bought it from and complain hard enough to get a refund, but I didn’t. I’m such a nice person.
* Fuel - Natural Selection  ★

  * Now this is a Fuel album I can get behind. It’s no Sunburn, which I will probably never find in the wild, but this band just shits gold imo so anything they produce is always a banger.
* Hajime Chitose - Kataritsugu Koto  ★

  * Good for weaboos, I wouldn’t describe you guys as the target audience for it at all though.
* Iron Maiden - The Number of the Beast

  * I don’t remember anything from this album but if I had to take a guess, like every other Iron Maiden song, it’s just 80’s metal religion and war slop, which might make someone out there really mad but I dunno I think listening to songs about eternal damnation and the brutalities of war to be a tragic thing to put my ears through.
* Jethro Tull - Thick as a Brick  ★

  * 10/10 go listen to it if you haven’t already. I even have it on vinyl where it’s just a whole ass newspaper customized to the themes of the album. I love how much of a shitpost the whole thing is, but each section reads as pretty distinct from eachother  
* Lil Jon & The East Side Boyz - Kings of Crunk  ★

  * Shit/10 but it’s super good to listen to with the windows down (go do that). I know virtually nothing about Lil Jon, but upon listening to this album cover to cover, I googled around and found that he had done a whole AMA on reddit a while ago. This dude is such a character, he only types with the caps lock on and it conveys his voice so well, I can never unhear it.
* Metallica - …And Justice for All  ★

  * If it wasn’t for One and the Metallica pinball machine, I wouldn’t have rated this too highly. I think what’s so good about it is how it starts off as not-so-much a Metallica song, and then it descends into pure madness at the end which ties into its themes so perfectly. There’s a reason why this was the first song of theirs to win a Grammy. The rest of it is shit, just like the band, that is only really saved by a few of their standouts which to me lacks real merit. I should’t have to buy their albums only to skip most of the garbage to listen to the real gold, it’s unAmerican to not consume everything.
* Metallica - Master of Puppets

  * Master of Puppets but every time there’s a bad song it gets faster.
* Naked Music - Carte Blanche Vol. 3  ★

  * Fun fact, I got this for like a dollar at my record store. I picked it up since the album art reminds me of the original Wii store in the best of ways. Anyways, pretty good, sounds like you’re on a psychedelic high while listening to it, it’s probably what hippies would listen to before they discovered shit like Nirvana.
* Nas - Hip Hop Is Dead  ★

  * I like when he said the N-word.
* Nelly Furtado - Whoa, Nelly!

  * I hate that they didn’t say the N-word.
* No Doubt - Tragic Kingdom  ★

  * White gurl music and that’s about it.
* Nujabes - Luv(sic) Hexalogy (feat. Shing02)  ★

  * Probably my favorite CD album to throw on if I didn’t readily have any regard as to what I wanted to listen to. Luv(sic) pt 3 is actually my favorite track from the album, but I do really love the neat additions in Disc 2 that has instrumental versions of all of the songs. Circling back to the first disc, it features remixed versions of each part after the first 6 parts, ending off with “Perfect Circle.” It’s just the perfect album, a staple for any CD collector.
* Nujabes - Metaphorical Music  ★

  * Quality elevator music/10.
* Nujabes - Modal Soul  ★

  * Fun fact, I either got a shitty copy or it was just manufactured this way, but it sounds so bass boosted whenever I put it on, it really perturbs me, but it’s probably the best Nujabes album if you put all of that aside. Good blend of genuine hip hop tracks with that Lofi style that Nujabes practically godfathered.
* Nujabes - Samurai Champloo: Departure  ★

  * I haven’t watched the anime but Shiki no Uta is fire.
* Paramore - Riot!

  * Similar to Avril Lavigne, Paramore has a few hits and that alone was paramount to their success, but the illusion of them being any good really falls apart when you actually give the full thing a listen. It all sounds the same, and unfortunately, Hayley Williams has a really whiny sounding voice after a while that really grinds my gears something fierce, that I don’t think I ever properly finished the whole thing. Good riddance to that.
* Shoji Meguro - Persona 3 Original Soundtrack

  * Eh.
* Shoji Meguro - Persona 4 Original Soundtrack  ★

  * Eh but it has it more ups than it has its downs. Ignoring the bias that I have where I think P4 Golden is the only Persona game worth playing, the music is more listenable outside of listening it inside the game’s vacuum. Signs of Love, Affection, Heartbeat Heartbreak, Heaven, are all really good standalone tracks, not really counting the rest of the songs in the album that doesn’t just come across as standard “video game music”. It’s great. I heard some news that the Revival game has a shittier soundtrack compared to this one, which is unfortunate but its kind of hard to top perfection. 
* Soul II Soul - Keep On Movin’

  * Uhhhh I forgot I had this one until I started writing this. I’m sure it has a good personality. 
* Sting - Brand New Day ★

  * Just Sting being Sting, I really like Desert Rose with some vague interest in some of the other tracks featured on here, but that’s about it. For some reason, I always encounter this CD in thrift stores, amongst all the Latino slop CDs that I always find whenever I go, which begs the question, does the Hispanic community praise Sting like they do Goku?
* Susumu Hirasawa - Berserk Original Soundtrack

  * My only encounter with Berserk is some random Portuguese copy of Volume 1 that I got at Goodwill and I have never read it but FYI rape isn’t cool guys.
* System of a Down - Toxicity  ★

  * This is the most beloved album by this band that for the sake of being contrarian, I’ll go out on a limb and say it sucks because of all the filler, and Chop Suey is so whatever. 
* Takeharu Ishimoto - The World Ends with You Original Soundtrack  ★

  * Probably the best video game soundtrack of all time. Don’t listen to Twister since it’ll probably dissuade you from it all until you give that song a listen enough times that it’ll trick you Pavlonian style into thinking it’s just okay, but the rest of the tracks are pretty good. It’s like Persona 3 but on drugs, and you might think some of the producers were while making some of these tracks, but it comes across as very well-made for a DS game of all mediums.
* The Cranberries - Everybody Else Is Doing It, So Why Can’t We?  ★

  * It’s like they took Linger and said “what if we made the same song but do it 12 more times?” And it’s somehow perfect.
* The Cranberries - To the Faithful Departed  ★

  * Probably the best Cranberries album and it’s not even close. You may say to me “But it doesn’t have Zombie” which yes, that may be true, but it has “I Just Shot John Lennon” wherein that song ends with gunshot sounds which I think is infinitely more valuable as a piece of media to the world. What did Dolores mean by this? Ignoring the context that it probably features some respectful message to his death, I didn’t like the guy, so R.I.P. to John Lennon but his death brought this song so maybe he died for no reason.
* The Pillows - FLCL Original Sound Track Vol. 3  ★

  * This is my favorite larping anime and it’s not even close (soz I’ll give up the parallelism). I only have the third volume of the sound track, which features, I would say, The Pillows to it’s fullest, as experienced in the anime. A lot of the tracks in the anime are instrumentals, with vocals being used sparingly and in some of the scenes, but there are tracks in Vol 3, like Carnival, that has the full vocals from whichever Pillows album they had originally pulled it from. It’s the perfect compilation album, and I highly recommend it for any other larpers out there that want to seem a touch more sophisticated with their psuedo taste in anime.
* The Police - Synchronicity  ★

  * I like the funny synchronicity and stalking song. This is just another Sting album where he’s using a lot of fake intellectualism to come off as smarter than everyone else. But I think the tracks work better here, it generally just sounds pleasant even when you turn off your brain for it. The band split up due to “creative differences” after this, but you can’t psyop me into believing it’s nothing more than Sting thinking his bandmates couldn’t catch up to him intellectually so he dropped them. 
* The Steve Miller Band - Greatest Hits 1974–78

  * Some people call me the space cowboy.

    * (it’s an okay album).
* U2 - Achtung Baby

  * U2 is probably one of the worst fucking mainstream bands out there and I’m tired of pretending they’re at least mediocre. And I don’t really want people telling me to go listen to other songs that probably hit their stride way better than this one did. This album is garbage, it all sounds the exact same and I struggle to think of one unique song by memory. Just awful. This band in particular always has a ton of CDs at the record store I go to, almost like nobody ever buys them, I wonder why?
